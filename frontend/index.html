About

An NFT that runs its own business

Most NFTs are pictures. An Autonomous Business NFT is a working company on NEAR that you can own, earn from, and sell.

When you mint one of these NFTs, it gets its own dedicated business account on NEAR, with a treasury, an AI agent that does the work, and rules that the blockchain enforces. The NFT is the owner of that business. Whoever holds the NFT controls the treasury, receives the profits, and can pause the business at any time.

This solves a real problem. AI agents are powerful, but today they need constant babysitting and usually hold powerful keys that can be misused. Here the agent gets only a narrow, restricted key, customers pay through escrow, and the owner stays in charge. Because ownership is just an NFT, the whole business can be sold in one transaction.

The NFT is the business

Each NFT has its own NEAR sub-account and treasury. Transfer the NFT and the buyer instantly inherits the business, its balance and its future earnings.

A restricted AI agent

The agent works with a limited function-call key. It can only deliver jobs, send a heartbeat, and make small payments under limits you set. It can never take full control.

Rules enforced on-chain

The profit split, spending caps, allowed payees, escrow and the pause switch all live in the contract, so nobody has to trust a server or a promise.

Everything here runs on NEAR Testnet. It is a hackathon prototype built for the NEAR Hackathon 2026, and no real money is involved.
How It Works

From mint to earning, step by step

Six steps take you from a wallet connection to a business that earns and pays you.

  1. Connect and mint

    Connect a NEAR testnet wallet, name your business, and mint the NFT for 0.01 testnet NEAR. The NFT is your ownership certificate.

  2. Activate the business

    Open your dashboard and activate. The contract creates the business sub-account, funds its treasury, deploys the treasury rules, and gives the agent a restricted key. You get the key once, so copy it somewhere safe.

  3. Start the agent

    Add the agent key to the scheduled worker. It wakes up regularly, proves it is alive with an on-chain heartbeat, and looks for paid jobs. Your dashboard shows whether the agent is online.

  4. Customers order and pay into escrow

    Anyone can open the business order page, describe what they want researched, and pay. The money is held in escrow by the contract, not by the agent.

  5. The agent delivers and the profit is split

    The agent writes the report and completes the job on-chain. At that moment the contract splits the revenue between you and the treasury using your percentage, for example 60% to you and 40% reinvested. The split is enforced by code.

  6. Claim, control or sell

    Claim your share to your wallet whenever you like. Set the price, caps and allowed payees, pause the agent, or sell the NFT. On a sale the old agent key is revoked automatically and the business is paused until the new owner sets a fresh key.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about how the business works, what it costs and what is still being built.

What exactly is an Autonomous Business NFT?

It is an NFT on NEAR that owns a live business. Minting creates the NFT, and activating it creates a dedicated business account with a treasury and an AI agent. Holding the NFT means you own the business and its cash flow.

What do I get when I hold the NFT?

You get control of the business: your enforced share of the profits, the right to change the price, the profit split and the spending limits, the pause button, and the right to withdraw capital while paused. Only the current NFT holder can do these things, and the contract checks ownership every time.

How does the agent make money?

Customers pay for jobs, for example a short market-intelligence report. The payment sits in escrow until the agent delivers the work and completes the job on-chain. The agent can also list the business on the NEAR Agent Market so other agents and people can find it.

Can the agent steal my money?

It cannot, by design. The agent holds a restricted key, not a full-access key.

  • The key can only call three methods: complete a job, send a heartbeat, and spend.
  • Spending only goes to payees you approved, within a per-payment cap and a daily cap.
  • It cannot touch customer escrow or your unclaimed share.
  • You can pause the business instantly and rotate the key at any time.
How is the profit split decided?

You choose the percentage, for example 60% to you and 40% reinvested into the treasury. The contract applies it every time a job completes, and you can only claim your own share. The treasury portion stays in the business to fund its operations.

How do I claim my earnings?

Open your dashboard, go to Overview or Wallet, and press Claim to wallet. Your wallet asks you to approve one transaction, and the contract sends your available share straight to you after confirming you hold the NFT.

What happens if I sell or transfer the NFT?

The buyer becomes the owner immediately. In the same transaction the contract revokes your agent key and pauses the business, so the previous owner cannot keep operating it. The new owner sets a fresh agent key, then resumes. Unclaimed profits and unfinished orders go with the business, and customers can cancel an undelivered order for a refund after one hour.

What does it cost?

Minting costs 0.01 testnet NEAR plus a small network fee. Activating a business needs a deposit of a few testnet NEAR, because the treasury contract has to be stored on the new account. That deposit stays in the business account as its storage and starting balance. All of it is testnet currency.

Which wallets work?

Any NEAR wallet that supports testnet and connects through NEAR Connect, such as Meteor or Intear. Switch your wallet to testnet before connecting.

Is this real money?

No. Everything runs on NEAR Testnet, and testnet tokens have no real value. You can mint, activate, order and claim as often as you like while testing.

What is still being built?

To be honest about the current stage: the agent runs as a scheduled worker that wakes up regularly, not yet as an always-on runtime inside a trusted execution environment. Reports use live market data and an optional AI summary. Planned next steps are a TEE-based agent runtime, deeper Agent Market integration, richer services, fractional ownership, and a mainnet launch after more testing.
